Two Voices

A day in the lives of Ronja and Daniel — told from two perspectives.

They don't know each other. They live in the same city, ride the same trams, walk the same bridges. One designs neighbourhoods. The other models energy systems. Their paths cross without either noticing.

This is one ordinary day, seen through two pairs of eyes.

The city hums on. Tomorrow will be different, and tomorrow will be the same. Two people, two rhythms, one place — endlessly overlapping, endlessly apart.
